# The Solar System

The Solar System consists of the Sun and everything that orbits around it, including eight planets, their moons, and millions of asteroids and comets.

## The Sun
The Sun is a star at the center of the Solar System. It provides light and heat to the planets.

## The Planets
- Mercury: The smallest planet and closest to the Sun.
- Venus: The second planet and the hottest in the Solar System.
- Earth: Our home planet, the only one known to support life.
- Mars: The Red Planet, home to the largest volcano in the Solar System.
